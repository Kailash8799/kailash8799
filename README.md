## About me

I'm a software engineer at **[Heizen](https://heizen.work)** (formerly OpenGig), where I work directly with clients to understand their problems, shape the product and take it from development to production. I've delivered **enterprise procurement platforms** for clients in Oman and India, and **two delivery apps live on Google Play and the App Store** with 3,000+ customers between them.

My focus is full-stack development across web and mobile: TypeScript, React, Next.js, Node.js, NestJS and React Native, with PostgreSQL, MongoDB and Redis, deployed on AWS and Google Cloud. I care about clean code, practical solutions and owning what I build.
