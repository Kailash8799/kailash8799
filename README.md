## About me

I'm a software engineer at **[Heizen](https://heizen.work)** (formerly OpenGig). I work directly with clients to understand their problems, shape the product and take it from development to production, for both enterprise clients and startups.

- **Enterprise platforms:** procurement systems for clients in Oman and India, including an AI inbox that reads supplier emails and links them to the right RFQ or purchase order.
- **Consumer apps:** two delivery apps live on Google Play and the App Store, with **3,000+ customers** between them.
- **Full stack:** TypeScript, React, Next.js, Node.js, NestJS and React Native, on PostgreSQL, MongoDB and Redis, deployed on AWS and Google Cloud.
- **How I work:** clean code, practical solutions and owning what I build, from the first conversation to production support.
